About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Utah, where I work with Prof. Fuheng Zhao on systems and formal-verification research. My interests span systems programming, model checking, LLVM instrumentation, and concurrency, and I have a strong background in robotics, embedded systems, and deep learning from my undergraduate work at MBM University, Jodhpur.

Before graduate school, I contributed to the open-source McMini model checker, developed autonomous robotics systems, and led large student teams — including mentoring 500+ students as student coordinator of the Embedded Systems & Robotics Club.
